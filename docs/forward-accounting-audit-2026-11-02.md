# Read-only forward accounting audit (2026-11-02)

`scripts/forward-accounting-audit.js` exports `auditSession(session, latestQuotes)` and `auditPair(pairedState, latestQuotes)`. It is pure: it reads no files, performs no network requests or account writes, invokes no Paper APIs, and never changes or substitutes the production clock. The consumer must first authenticate the immutable journal/packet chain and pass the quotes from its final packet; a self-consistent hash does not authenticate market facts.

The supported scope is deliberately narrow: the preregistered forward-manager-paired-20261102-a pair, six fixed ETFs, independent CNY50,000 sessions, fixed candidate identities and decision policy hashes, live forward session declaration, original default fees and 25%/12500/three-position/four-percent rules. Both dates must be in the registered known-session window; unknown 2027 dates and historical pseudo-forward snapshots reject. Corporate events, receivables and dividends are unsupported and reject rather than being filled with zero.

The audit independently reduces recorded **model-account** fills from initial cash. Rejected orders never supply proceeds. It recomputes price-times-quantity, ETF commission/minimum charges, total cash, paid fees, entry fee allocation, remaining lots, realized profits, terminal marked value and equity. Closed fragments must refer to their original buy order through researchLotId; research reductions obey eligible FIFO and T+1. Risk/maturity fills identify a complete specific lot, so a stop on a later lot is not falsely treated as a FIFO research reduction. Final quote/source fields and the paired observation timestamp must match the final sessions. Comparison-account orders are outside this model-account audit and do not enter model cash.

`accountingReplay:true` means **recorded-order arithmetic reconstruction only**. `paperReplay:false` always remains: this does not regenerate trading plans, stop/expiry intentions, order acceptance decisions, adverse slippage prices, quote freshness at each historic fill, price limit executability or complete Paper state transitions. It cannot certify source authenticity, justify returns, authorize launch, reset an account, or grant a strategy promotion. Those flags remain false. It also does not replace the journal's full immutable-chain audit or separately authenticated corporate-event monitoring.

Validation: 11 new accounting targets plus six imported existing pure-kernel fixture tests = 17 passing tests under `--max-old-space-size=192`. Tests include a three-tick actual pure-engine fixture with same-day blocked exits and next-session stop fills, partial fee allocation/FIFO, a later-lot risk exit, rejected sale proceeds, altered fees/cash/shares/closed fragments/risk policy, missing valuation source, pair clock mismatch and out-of-window/unknown-calendar identities. All Paper clock overrides occur solely in pre-existing isolated synthetic test fixture helpers; the new audit module has no clock override or Paper dependency. Red/green and test-fixture construction failures are retained under D:/techno/validation/daily-20261102/forward-accounting-audit. No real account, queue, worker or scheduler changed.
